RateWall
A limit of 5 per second can let 10 through in a blink.
Open the app
Five requests at the end of one second and five at the start of the next all pass a fixed window limit of 5 per second: 10 requests in under 200 ms. A token bucket or sliding log lets 5.

What it does

  • Five policies on one trace, shown as a timeline of allowed and denied requests
  • Token bucket, GCRA and leaky bucket meter give identical decisions
  • Shows the fixed window boundary burst
  • Retry-after, refill time and burst facts for the token bucket
Policies follow the Wikipedia descriptions; denied requests are dropped. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
